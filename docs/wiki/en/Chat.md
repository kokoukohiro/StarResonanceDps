This widget shows the game's chat in one window per channel or per private chat partner.

## How to open

- On the "Chat" card in "[Widgets](Widgets)", choose from the drop-down list in the name field and click the start button. World, Guild, Team, Current, and Newbie come first, followed by private chat partners from the newest (the default is World).
- Private chat partners appear after you log in to the game or after a private chat arrives.
- A window is opened per channel or partner. If the same window is already open, that window is brought to the front.

## What it shows

- Title: The channel name, as in "Chat - World", or "Chat - Private Chat (partner's name)".
- Messages: The first line shows the name and the time (in the form "21:40"), and the text follows from the second line. The text wraps at the width of the window. Your own messages are also shown.
- Names: They follow "Player name" in Settings. For announcements that have no sender, only the time is shown.
- Text
  - Emoticons are shown as emoji. They use the Windows emoji designs, which differ from the game's images.
  - URLs are underlined links.
  - Stickers are shown as images. Stickers with no image show "[Sticker]".
  - Voice messages show "[Voice Message]" (followed by the text, if there is any).
  - Game links such as Personal Space, Master Seal, and fishing records are shown with the same text as in the game (they cannot be clicked). When they cannot be turned into text, they show "[Link]". Player names inside links are not hidden by the "Player name" setting.
- Order: Older messages are at the top. While you are viewing the bottom, the view follows new messages.

## Controls

- Drag over text to select it across messages. Shift+click extends the selection, and clicking clears it. If you drag above or below the window or turn the mouse wheel while dragging, the view scrolls.
- When text is selected, right-click to choose "Copy". While the window is active, you can also copy with Ctrl+C.
- In copied text, each message has "name time" followed by the text on the next line. Emoticons become emoji characters, and stickers become "[Sticker]".
- Click a URL to open it in your default browser.
- Scroll with the mouse wheel or the scroll bar on the right.

## Settings

The window settings and theme are described in [Common Widget Settings](Common-Widget-Settings).

### Text Colors

The text colors of Player name, Time, and Message (the text). The Message color does not apply to URLs.

## Notes

- The app can receive only the chat that the game loads. After you log in, the game loads the newest 30 messages for each channel and the last message for each private chat partner. The full private chat arrives when you open the chat with that partner in the game.
- If you start the app after logging in to the game, some past chat may be missing (the circle in the window title is yellow). For World and Newbie, switching channels in the game fixes it; for private chats, opening the chat with that partner in the game fixes it; and for Guild, Team, and Current, logging in again fixes it.
- The contents are cleared only when you log out (when you return to character selection or the login screen). They are not cleared when you change maps.
- The contents are not saved to a file, so they are cleared when you close the app.
- The game's "General", "Gameplay", and "System" channels and the announcements shown at the top of the screen are not shown.
- "Private Chat" and "World Chat" in [Notifications](Notifications) notify even when this window is not open.
