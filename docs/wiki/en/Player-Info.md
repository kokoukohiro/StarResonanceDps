This widget shows one player's level, location, ability score, season strength, class, Deep-Slumber Psychoscope, and more in one place.

## How to open

- On the "Player Info" card in "[Widgets](Widgets)", choose the target from the drop-down list in the name field and click the start button. The default target is yourself.
- You can also open it by clicking a row in the [Player List](Player-List) or the [Revive List](Revive-List), or by right-clicking a row and choosing "Player Info".
- A window is opened per target. If a window for the same target is already open, that window is brought to the front.

## What it shows

- Title: "Player Info - player name".
- Top left: The avatar photo, the player name, and the UID.
- Five lines below the avatar: Adventurer Level (the season level is in parentheses), Season Title, Location, Ability Score, and Season Strength. Values that are not known show "Unknown".
- Three icons below the five lines: The current season, the class spec (such as Block Spec), and the Deep-Slumber Psychoscope type. "Class I" appears if the player has no talents equipped, "Psychoscope Not Enabled" if the Deep-Slumber Psychoscope is not enabled, and a question mark and "Unknown" if it is not known.
- Right: The namecard photo.
- The avatar and namecard photos appear when the game has sent the photos. Otherwise, the default images are shown. For players who have chosen an icon provided by the game, that icon is shown (avatar only).

## Controls

- Right-click your own avatar or namecard photo to choose "Save Image As…" or "Copy Image". This is available only while a photo is shown.

## Settings

The window settings and theme are described in [Common Widget Settings](Common-Widget-Settings). There are no display settings. Names follow "Player name" in [Settings](Settings).

### Icon Colors

Choose the colors of the three icons below the five lines for each class (including transformations and unknown), each Deep-Slumber Psychoscope type (including Psychoscope Not Enabled), and each season.

## Notes

- The class spec and Deep-Slumber Psychoscope of other players become known when they come near you. They cannot be known from namecard or party information.
- The avatar and namecard photos of other players arrive when you open a screen in the game that shows their avatar (chat, the friend list, namecards, and so on) and while they are party members. Photos are not saved to files; they are loaded from the URLs sent by the game when the window opens.
- The location is known only for people around you and for party members. After you restart the app, the location of party members may not be known until you open the party screen.
- For people who have left the Player List (for example, people who moved away from you), the last known values remain, and only the location changes to "Unknown". They are cleared when you log out.
- If you start the app while the game is already running, the season and season title are not known until you change maps, and your own level, ability score, and season strength show "Unknown".
- While a player is transformed, the ability score and season strength are the transformed values sent by the game.
- While a history entry is open, the season, season title, class, Deep-Slumber Psychoscope, and photos from that time are shown for the people who were in that battle.
- The window size is not saved, and the window opens at the default size every time. The position is saved.
