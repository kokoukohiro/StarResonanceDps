This widget lists the node effects, advanced node effects, and factors that are active in one player's Deep-Slumber Psychoscope, one per row.

## How to open

- On the "Deep-Slumber Psychoscope Info" card in "[Widgets](Widgets-en)", choose the target from the drop-down list in the name field and click the start button. The default target is yourself.
- You can also open it by right-clicking a row in the [Player List](Player-List-en) or the [Revive List](Revive-List-en) and choosing "Deep-Slumber Psychoscope Info".
- A window is opened per target. If a window for the same target is already open, that window is brought to the front.

## What it shows

- Title: "Deep-Slumber Psychoscope Info - player name".
- Row: The icon on the left and the name on the right. They are listed in the order node effects → advanced node effects → factors.
- The names of advanced node effects are "General 1" to "General 5", and their icons differ by type.
- Factor names: For other players, they are shown without the grade (such as "Heavy Guardian Reality Factor X6"); for yourself, they are shown with the grade.
- When there is nothing to show, the window is empty.

## Controls

- Hovering over a row highlights it. Clicking does nothing.
- If the rows do not fit in the window, scroll with the mouse wheel or the scroll bar on the right.

## Settings

There are only the window settings and theme ([Common Widget Settings](Common-Widget-Settings-en)).

## Notes

- For other players, the entries are taken from the buffs that the effects apply. Therefore, factors that apply no buff are not shown, and the grades of factors are not known.
- Your own factors are shown with their grades, including factors that apply no buff, from your Deep-Slumber Psychoscope progression data. If you start the app while the game is already running, they are shown the same way as for other players until you change maps.
- The type itself is not shown (it appears in the [Player List](Player-List-en) and [Player Info](Player-Info-en)). The Illusion-Breaking Psychoscope and the type-specific effects of advanced nodes are not shown either.
- Other players' data arrives while they are around you. It does not arrive through party information.
- For people who have left the Player List, the last known values remain. They are cleared when you log out.
- While a history entry is open, the values from that time are shown for the people who were in that battle.
