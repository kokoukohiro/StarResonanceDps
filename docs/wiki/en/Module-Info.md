This widget lists one player's module effects, one effect per row.

## How to open

- On the "Module Info" card in "[Widgets](Widgets)", choose the target from the drop-down list in the name field and click the start button. The default target is yourself.
- You can also open it by right-clicking a row in the [Player List](Player-List) or the [Revive List](Revive-List) and choosing "Module Info".
- A window is opened per target. If a window for the same target is already open, that window is brought to the front.

## What it shows

- Title: "Module Info - player name".
- Row: The effect icon on the left and the effect name on the right.
- Yourself: All effects of the equipped modules are shown with their level (such as "Lv6") on the right, in order from the highest level. Effects whose link count has not arrived show "Lv??".
- Other players: Only the Lv5 and Lv6 effects that apply a buff in the game are shown. Levels are not shown.
- When there is nothing to show, the window is empty.

## Controls

- Hovering over a row highlights it. Clicking does nothing.
- If the rows do not fit in the window, scroll with the mouse wheel or the scroll bar on the right.

## Settings

There are only the window settings and theme ([Common Widget Settings](Common-Widget-Settings)).

## Notes

- What is known differs between yourself and other players. Other players' modules are not sent by the game, so only what can be learned from the buffs that the effects apply is shown.

| | Yourself | Other players |
|---|---|---|
| Effects shown | All effects of the equipped modules | Only Lv5 and Lv6 effects that apply a buff in the game |
| Level | Shown (such as "Lv6") | Not shown (whether it is Lv5 or Lv6 is not known either) |

- Effects that apply no buff ("Strength Boost", "Resistance", "Armor", "Cast Focus", "Attack SPD", "Crit Focus", and "Luck Focus") are not shown for other players, regardless of their level.
- Your own effects are built from the data that arrives when you log in or change maps, and they change immediately when you change modules. If you start the app while the game is already running, they are shown the same way as for other players until you change maps.
- Other players' data arrives while they are around you. It does not arrive through party information.
- For people who have left the Player List, the last known values remain. They are cleared when you log out.
- While a history entry is open, the effects from that time are shown without levels for the people who were in that battle.
