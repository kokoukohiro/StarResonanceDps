This tab shows the record of what the app is doing (the log).

## What it shows

- Each line is one record. From the left, it shows the time, the level, and the content.
- Level: INF (information), WRN (warning), ERR (error), and so on.
- New records are added at the bottom. While you are viewing the bottom, the view follows new records.

## Controls

- Drag over text to select it (you can select across lines). You can copy the selected text with "Copy" on the right-click menu or Ctrl+C.
- Scroll with the mouse wheel or the scroll bar.

## Notes

- This tab shows only the newest 500 records. All records are in `Data\CombatRuntime.log` in the app folder.
- The records are written in English.
- The log is deleted when the app version changes.
- If you are asked for the log in a bug report, attach `Data\CombatRuntime.log` ([Feedback](Feedback)).
